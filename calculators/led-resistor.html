What is an LED Resistor?

An LED resistor limits the current flowing through an LED. Without suitable current limiting, the LED can be damaged.

LED Resistor Formula

R = (Vs - Vf) ÷ I

Variables

  • Vs = Supply voltage
  • Vf = LED forward voltage
  • I = LED current
  • R = Series resistor

Example

Suppose you have a 5 V supply, a red LED with a forward voltage of 2 V, and you want 20 mA.

R = (5 - 2) ÷ 0.020

R = 150 Ω

Common LED Forward Voltages

  • Red LED: approximately 1.8 to 2.2 V
  • Yellow LED: approximately 2.0 to 2.2 V
  • Green LED: approximately 2.0 to 3.2 V
  • Blue LED: approximately 2.8 to 3.4 V
  • White LED: approximately 2.8 to 3.4 V

Resistor Power

The resistor also dissipates power. After calculating the resistor value, check its power rating.

P = I² × R
