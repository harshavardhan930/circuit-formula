What is dBm?

dBm is a logarithmic power ratio unit referenced to 1 milliwatt (mW). It is the industry standard for expressing RF signal levels, wireless link budgets, transmit powers, and receiver sensitivities.

dBm to Watt Formula

P(W) = 10(P(dBm) / 10) × 0.001 = 10((P(dBm) - 30) / 10)

Watt to dBm Formula

P(dBm) = 10 × log₁₀(P(W) / 0.001) = 10 × log₁₀(P(W)) + 30

dBW Relationship

P(dBW) = P(dBm) - 30

Where dBm is Used

  • RF Transceivers (e.g., CC1101, SX1276, ESP32 Wi-Fi/BLE)
  • Antenna link budget calculations and path loss modeling
  • Spectrum analyzers and RF power meters
  • Low Noise Amplifiers (LNA) and Power Amplifiers (PA)
