What is an RC Time Constant?

The RC time constant describes how quickly a capacitor charges or discharges through a resistor.

The symbol for the time constant is τ, pronounced "tau".

RC Time Constant Formula

τ = R × C

What Happens During Charging?

After one time constant, the capacitor reaches approximately 63.2% of its final voltage.

After five time constants, the capacitor reaches approximately 99.3% of its final voltage.

Common RC Timing Points

  • 1τ = 63.2%
  • 2τ = 86.5%
  • 3τ = 95.0%
  • 4τ = 98.2%
  • 5τ = 99.3%

Capacitor Charging Formula

Vc = Vs × (1 - e-t/RC)

This formula calculates the capacitor voltage during charging.
